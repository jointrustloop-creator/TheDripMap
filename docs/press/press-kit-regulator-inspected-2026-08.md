## 1. The one-line story

A small Canadian directory started publishing what IV therapy clinics'
regulators actually say about them, including inspection outcomes from
Ontario's public IVIT Premises Register that most patients have never
heard of.

## 2. Why it is a story now

- IV therapy lounges have grown fast across Canada with uneven oversight,
  and mainstream coverage has focused on the risks without giving
  consumers a practical way to check a specific clinic.
- Ontario's College of Naturopaths (CONO) maintains a public, searchable
  IVIT Premises Register recording which ND-led premises hold current
  authorization for intravenous infusion therapy. Almost no consumers know
  it exists.
- TheDripMap now mirrors those outcomes on clinic listings (only positive,
  current authorizations render publicly) and teaches readers to run the
  same checks themselves at /verification.
- Angle for journalists: "here is the free public register you did not
  know existed, and here is the site that checks it for you."

## 3. Fact sheet (verified 2026-08-15, re-check before sending)

- 1,612 active clinic listings on TheDripMap; 619 in Canada.
- 27 clinics claimed by their owners; 10 hold the Safety Verified badge,
  which requires an operator to check the named prescriber against the
  live public register (CPSO, CNO, or CONO) before it renders. The badge
  is human-approved, never automatic.
- 3 Ontario clinics currently display "Regulator-inspected premises,"
  mirroring a current authorization on CONO's public IVIT Premises
  Register: Insight Naturopathic Clinic, Nature's Touch Naturopathic
  Clinic, and Soma and Soul Wellness (all Toronto area).
- 76 drip menu items captured verbatim from clinic-published menus with
  source URL and capture date on every record; 53 carry a published price.
  We never paraphrase a clinic's menu or add health claims to it.
- Verification ladder (docs/badge-standard.md): L1 owner-provided is never
  called "verified"; L2 register-checked is the badge; L5 mirrors regulator
  premises inspections. Negative or absent register results never render
  publicly; they become internal review items.
- To our knowledge, TheDripMap is the only Canadian IV therapy directory
  publishing regulator premises-inspection outcomes on clinic listings.
