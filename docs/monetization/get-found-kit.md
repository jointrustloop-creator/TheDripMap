1. Listing optimization pass: rewritten description, services menu, and photos
   checklist for their TheDripMap page, applied for them.
2. Local visibility mini-audit: how they show up for "[city] IV therapy"
   on TheDripMap + a Google Business Profile tune-up checklist (categories,
   services, photos, review prompts).

   IV Price Index (where index data exists).
4. One "limited-time offer" set up on their listing + city page (the deals
   module) with copy written for them.

generator + /tools/seo-audit + Price Index data), 20% manual polish. Target
under 45 minutes of operator time per kit.

3 months of Featured at $99 (post-founding rate) as a closer.

basics, honestly delivered.
