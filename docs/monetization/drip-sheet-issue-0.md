**1. Regulators are looking closer at IVIT.**
Ontario's College of Naturopaths runs a premises inspection program for IV
therapy, and the badge-worthy question every clinic should be able to answer
is who starts the IV and under whose oversight. Our plain-language guides by
province: Ontario, BC, Alberta, Quebec (links on thedripmap.com/blog). If your
front desk can answer those two questions, you are ahead of most.

**2. Your prices are public data now.**
Our IV Price Index tracks published menu prices across cities. Toronto right
now: a standard drip runs CA$119 to CA$399, median CA$175, across 17 clinics'
published menus. Calgary and Edmonton are live too. If your pricing sits far
off the median, patients comparing clinics can see it, and so can you:
thedripmap.com/iv-prices/toronto

**3. What patients clicked last month.**
Across our Canadian listings in the last 30 days: website clicks nearly 5x'd
versus the prior month, and Montreal and Toronto produced more than half of
all patient actions. If you are in a smaller city, you have less competition
for the same intent. Claiming your listing is free and takes two minutes.
