# TheDripMap Consumer Newsletter — reusable monthly template

Audience: the newsletter subscribers (consumers interested in IV therapy, NOT
clinic owners). Promise made at signup: "One short email a month. No spam, no
upsells. Just new clinics, pricing changes, and treatment guides for your city."
Cadence: monthly. Express consent under CASL (cleanest send).

Each issue takes ~1 hour: pick 3 live links + a find-a-clinic line + send.

---

## Structure (fill the 4 slots)

**Subject:** short, honest, no hype. Pattern: "IV therapy in Canada, minus the
hype — [month] update".

**Intro (2 lines):** one friendly line + the reassurance. First issue only:
acknowledge the gap ("you signed up a while back…"). Later issues: skip that.

**Three things worth reading this month (the core):** three LIVE links, each one
line of why-it-matters. Rotate from:
- A regulatory/practical post (province rules, insurance, who-can-give-an-IV).
- A cost/data asset (an /iv-prices/[city] page, ideally one refreshed that month).
- A treatment explainer (NAD+, glutathione, iron infusion, vitamin C) — honest,
  evidence-caveated.
Verify all three return 200 before sending.

**Find a clinic near you (1 line + link):** point to /search or a specific city
page that got new clinics that month ("new this month in [city]").

**Footer:** "We'll keep this to about once a month… unsubscribe: reply
'unsubscribe' and you're off the list immediately." Signed Hubert.
