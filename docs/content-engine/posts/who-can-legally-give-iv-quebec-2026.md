Quebec plays by different rules than the rest of Canada when it comes to who can legally give you an IV, and the difference matters when you are choosing a clinic in Montreal or Quebec City. In Quebec, administering an IV is reserved to specific licensed professionals, and the naturopathic route that exists in some other provinces does not apply here.

This guide explains, in plain language, who can legally give you an IV in Quebec in 2026. It is written for patients choosing a safe clinic, not for operators. It is general information, not legal or medical advice, and the rules can change. Always confirm current requirements with the relevant Quebec regulatory order and with the clinician in front of you. ## The short answer

In Quebec, the people who can legally start and run a wellness IV for you are:

- **Physicians (medecins)**, regulated by the College des medecins du Quebec (CMQ)
- **Nurses (infirmieres and infirmiers)**, regulated by the Ordre des infirmieres et infirmiers du Quebec (OIIQ), who can insert a peripheral IV and administer prescribed substances under an order or medical directive

What makes Quebec structurally different: naturopathy is not a regulated health profession in the province. That means there is no Quebec equivalent of the naturopathic IV authorization that exists in Ontario, BC, and Alberta. A naturopath cannot legally start an IV on you in Quebec.

## Why Quebec is different

In Ontario, British Columbia, and Alberta, a naturopathic doctor with the right provincial certification can administer a defined list of IV substances. Quebec has no regulatory college for naturopathy, so "naturopaths" there are not a regulated profession and are not authorized to perform the reserved act of starting an IV. In Quebec, the reserved activities around IV therapy sit with nurses and physicians.

Federally, [Health Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-health-products.html) still governs the drugs and natural health products that can be compounded or administered by IV, the same as everywhere else in Canada. So in Quebec the two questions are: is the person a nurse or physician acting within their authorized scope, and is the substance legal to administer.

## Nurses and physicians in Quebec

Most legitimate IV clinics in Quebec run on nursing and physician credentials. A nurse registered with the [Ordre des infirmieres et infirmiers du Quebec](https://www.oiiq.org/en/) may insert a peripheral IV and administer substances intravenously under the appropriate prescription or medical directive. A physician registered with the [College des medecins du Quebec](http://www.cmq.org/) can prescribe and administer, and often serves as the prescriber or medical director behind a clinic's protocols.

One Quebec-specific detail worth knowing: auxiliary nurses (regulated by a separate order, the OIIAQ) do not administer medications by the intravenous route. So the person starting and running your drip should be a nurse from the OIIQ or a physician, not an auxiliary nurse or an unregulated technician.

## Who cannot legally start your IV in Quebec

- Naturopaths, since naturopathy is not a regulated profession in Quebec
- Aestheticians or medical-spa staff who are not also nurses or physicians
- Auxiliary nurses acting outside their scope
- Wellness coaches, "IV technicians," or any unregulated practitioner

If a Quebec clinic cannot clearly tell you that a nurse or physician starts your IV, treat that as a reason to pause.

## What to check before you book in Quebec

- **Ask who starts the IV** and confirm they are an OIIQ nurse or a CMQ physician.
- **Ask who prescribes or acts as medical director.**
- **Ask what is in the bag** and whether it is compounded by a licensed pharmacy.
- **Be skeptical of cure or detox claims.** IV vitamin therapy is a legitimate wellness service, not a disease treatment.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can a naturopath give you an IV in Quebec?

No. Naturopathy is not a regulated health profession in Quebec, so there is no naturopathic IV authorization like the ones in Ontario, BC, and Alberta. In Quebec, starting an IV is reserved to nurses registered with the OIIQ and physicians registered with the CMQ. A naturopath cannot legally start an IV on you in the province.

### Who can legally give an IV in Montreal?

The same rules apply across Quebec, including Montreal: a nurse from the Ordre des infirmieres et infirmiers du Quebec or a physician from the College des medecins du Quebec, working under an appropriate prescription or medical directive. Ask a Montreal clinic to confirm the credential of the person starting your drip.

### Do you need a doctor on site for IV therapy in Quebec?

Not necessarily a physician in the room, but there must be a qualified prescriber behind the treatment and a nurse or physician performing it. Many clinics use a physician medical director who sets the protocols that OIIQ nurses follow under a medical directive. What matters is that a licensed prescriber stands behind what you receive.

### Is IV vitamin therapy legal in Quebec?

Yes. Wellness IV therapy is legal in Quebec when it is performed by an OIIQ nurse or a CMQ physician using ingredients that are legal to compound and administer. The regulation is about who performs the reserved act and what is in the bag, not a ban on the service.

### What should I ask a clinic before my first IV in Quebec?

Ask whether a nurse or physician starts the IV, who prescribes or acts as medical director, what is in the drip and where it is compounded, and how they screen you for contraindications. Because Quebec does not regulate naturopathy, confirming a nursing or physician credential is especially worth doing here.
