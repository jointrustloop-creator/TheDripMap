# Press target list — Canadian IV Therapy Report (PLAN-4)

Built 2026-09-01. Rules of engagement:
- Sends happen ONLY after Hubert approves the pitch format (Gmail draft
  "[TEST, format review] Press pitch for the Canadian IV Therapy Report").
- One personalized email per outlet, sent individually from info@ (never a
  blast, never CC), Tue/Wed morning ET. Max 8 to 10 per day so replies get
  same-day human attention.
- Every pitch localizes the data: the outlet's city gets its own clinic
  count and, where the price index covers it, its own price range.
- No follow-up nag; one send, one optional value-add follow-up after 5
  business days IF the report gains a new stat worth sharing.
- Tier 1 goes first; Tier 2 only after we see how Tier 1 lands.
